# Founder & Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

## Shariful Islam

Shariful Islam is a Bangladeshi Engineer, Ethical Visionary, and Entrepreneur dedicated to ethical innovation, humanitarian development, knowledge-driven progress, and civilization-focused institution building.

Born in 2001 in Jamalpur, Bangladesh, he developed strong interests in technology, engineering, Islamic ethics, global affairs, media and journalism, education, research, humanitarian development, renewable energy, and future civilization studies from an early age.

He completed a Bachelor of Engineering (B.E.) in Electrical Engineering (E.E.) from The University of Burdwan, India. His academic and intellectual interests include:
- electrical engineering,
- smart power grid systems,
- solar and photovoltaic (PV) technology,
- renewable and green energy,
- artificial intelligence,
- ethical technology systems,
- international business and relations,
- media and journalism,
- humanitarian development,
- Islamic civilization,
- education and research,
- and future-focused ethical innovation.

Shariful Islam is inspired by Islamic ethics, Prophetic principles, humanitarian responsibility, and the legacy of past Muslim scholars, scientists, philosophers, reformers, and the Sahabah of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ. Among contemporary figures, he has also been influenced by Dr. Zakir Naik and Imran Khan, the former Prime Minister of Pakistan.

After observing corruption, exploitation, unethical corporate systems, environmental destruction, social imbalance, and the growing separation between morality and modern development, he envisioned creating an institution that combines faith, ethics, knowledge, technology, transparency, and humanitarian responsibility within one balanced ecosystem.

From this vision, The Muslim Company was founded in January 2025 as a civilization-focused ethical global enterprise inspired by Islamic ethics, Prophetic principles, humanitarian responsibility, and ethical civilization-building.

As Founder & CEO, Shariful Islam seeks to help build:
- ethical and transparent business systems,
- halal economic ecosystems,
- humanitarian development platforms,
- knowledge and research institutions,
- ethical technology systems,
- sustainable infrastructure,
- environmental protection initiatives,
- and long-term civilization-focused global frameworks.

He believes business should not exist only for profit, but should also contribute toward:
- human dignity,
- education,
- innovation,
- justice,
- worker welfare,
- environmental responsibility,
- social stability,
- and long-term benefit for humanity.

Shariful Islam also believes that technology and development must remain under ethical guidance and should never harm morality, family, society, mental well-being, nature, or the creation of Allah.

His long-term aspiration is to help build one of the world’s most trusted, respected, and influential ethical Muslim-led global enterprises through halal, transparent, humanity-centered, and civilization-focused systems.

At the spiritual foundation of his vision is the belief that true success is not only wealth, fame, or power, but becoming accepted by Allah and leaving behind long-term benefit for humanity.

InshaAllah.
