# The Muslim Company – Our Story

## From Observation, Pain & Responsibility to a Civilization Vision

Shariful Islam spent much of his early life observing society, people, technology, education, economics, media, and the changing direction of the modern world. Over time, he began to realize that although humanity was advancing technologically, many societies were simultaneously moving away from morality, humanity, justice, and true peace.

He observed that many large corporations and institutions around the world often focus only on profit, market domination, and power while ignoring:
- morality,
- human dignity,
- workers’ rights,
- environmental balance,
- family stability,
- spiritual well-being,
- ethical responsibility,
- and the long-term consequences of their actions.

In many cases, he saw systems where:
- people are exploited through addiction-based business models,
- misleading marketing manipulates public emotions,
- workers are underpaid and treated unfairly,
- corruption and bribery control markets,
- environmental destruction is normalized for profit,
- technology is used more for control than for human benefit,
- knowledge is commercialized but true education is neglected,
- and powerful institutions continue growing stronger while ordinary people struggle behind.

At the same time, he also noticed that practicing Muslims often face difficulties finding ethical professional environments where faith, dignity, modesty, and professionalism can coexist peacefully.

In many places:
- visible Islamic identity is discouraged,
- halal professional ecosystems are limited,
- ethical workplace culture is weak,
- modest and respectful work systems for women are rare,
- Muslim youth lack organized opportunities for skill development, innovation, leadership, and research,
- and Islamic values are often treated as obstacles rather than strengths.

These realities deeply affected his thinking.

Over time, he began asking himself:

“Can there not be a global institution built upon ethics, transparency, halal economics, knowledge, research, technology, humanitarian responsibility, and Prophetic principles?”

“Can a company not exist that people can trust completely without fear of exploitation?”

“Can business not work for humanity, justice, education, ethical development, environmental balance, and civilization-building instead of existing only for profit?”

These questions slowly became the foundation of The Muslim Company.

---

# A Vision Beyond Business

The Muslim Company was never intended to become only a traditional business corporation.

Its vision was always much larger.

The goal was to build:
- an ethical business ecosystem,
- a halal economic model,
- a humanitarian development platform,
- a knowledge and research revival movement,
- an ethical technology ecosystem,
- a transparent and accountable institution,
- and a civilization-focused global enterprise.

The company seeks to:
- support humanity ethically,
- reduce poverty and unemployment,
- create skill and employment opportunities,
- protect workers’ dignity,
- support education and healthcare,
- protect nature and Allah’s creation,
- develop ethical technologies,
- and contribute toward long-term civilization development.

# The Spiritual Foundation Behind Everything

However, behind all of these plans, visions, dreams, and ambitions, the most important foundation is intention (niyyah).

Shariful Islam believes:

“I do not know whether this company will fully succeed, how far it will go, or whether I will be able to implement every part of this vision. But my intention is to seek the pleasure of Allah and, by following the teachings and ethics of Prophet Muhammad ﷺ as much as possible, try to build something beneficial for humanity.”

He also believes:

“True success is not only wealth, fame, or power. True success is becoming accepted by Allah and leaving behind benefit for humanity.”

For this reason, The Muslim Company does not claim perfection.

Instead, the company seeks to:
- correct mistakes,
- accept truth,
- protect ethics,
- maintain accountability,
- serve humanity,
- and prioritize the pleasure of Allah above everything else.

---

# Final Philosophy

The Muslim Company believes:

“Human beings plan, work, and struggle, but ultimate success, rizq, honor, and results belong only to Allah.”

The company also believes:

“Any development that destroys humanity, morality, family, nature, or the guidance of Allah is not true development.”

For this reason, The Muslim Company seeks to help build a future where:
- business exists,
- technology advances,
- research grows,
- and innovation flourishes,

—but where ethics, humanity, justice, and the pleasure of Allah remain at the center of everything.

InshaAllah.
