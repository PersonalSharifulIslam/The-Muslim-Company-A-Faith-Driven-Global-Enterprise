The Muslim Company Foundation

Serving Humanity, Protecting Creation — For the Sake of Allah

The Muslim Company Foundation is the dedicated humanitarian and environmental arm of The Muslim Company — channeling zakat, sadaqah, waqf, and 10% of monthly net profit directly into the welfare of every creation of Allah. We work to meet the basic needs of street children, orphans, and the poor — food, clothing, shelter, education, and healthcare — while also caring for animals, birds, and all living creatures, and for the trees, plants, and natural world entrusted to us, across every country we operate in.

"And they give food, in spite of love for it, to the needy, the orphan, and the captive."

— Quran 76:8

Our Purpose

One institution, two missions: build ethical enterprise, and serve those in need.

Every sector of The Muslim Company — from technology to manufacturing to finance — exists not only to operate ethically, but to fund a permanent channel of good. The Foundation is that channel: caring not only for human beings, but for every creature Allah has created — animals, birds, insects, and wildlife — and for the earth itself, its trees, plants, soil, and water.

The Prophet ﷺ taught that mercy extends to every living being — describing how a man was forgiven for giving water to a thirsty dog, and how a woman was held accountable for letting a cat starve. Guided by this model, the Foundation operates with full accountability under the company's Supreme Shariah Board and publishes its activity through The Muslim Company's transparency reporting.

Areas of Work

Six pillars through which the Foundation serves humanity and creation.

Humanitarian Relief

Emergency disaster response, refugee assistance, and food security programs for the most vulnerable communities.

Street Children & Poverty Relief

Direct support for street children and impoverished families — meeting basic needs of food, clothing, and shelter, alongside pathways to education and a safe upbringing.

Orphan & Widow Care

Sponsorship, housing support, and long-term welfare programs for orphans and widows across our operating regions.

Healthcare Access

Medical camps, essential treatment support, and health education for underserved populations.

Education & Scholarships

Scholarships, school supplies, and literacy programs to break cycles of poverty through knowledge.

Environmental & Plant Life

Reforestation, tree planting, and ecological restoration — honoring the earth, its soil, and every living plant as a trust from Allah.

Animal & Wildlife Welfare

Protection, feeding, rescue, and habitat preservation for animals, birds, and all creatures — following the Prophetic teaching that kindness to every living being is an act of worship.

Clean Water & Sanitation

Wells, water purification, and sanitation infrastructure for communities without reliable access — for people, livestock, and wildlife alike.

Full Accountability

Every Foundation activity is reviewed by the Supreme Shariah Board and reported publicly through The Muslim Company's annual transparency reports — covering fund distribution, zakat calculation, and humanitarian outcomes.

Global Reach

The Foundation extends across every country where The Muslim Company operates and has provided relief across Bangladesh, India, Pakistan, the United Arab Emirates, Nigeria, Ghana, and Gambia — with support from donors across multiple countries and more regions planned as the company grows.

Legal Structure

The Muslim Company Foundation is being formally registered as a non-profit organization, controlled by The Muslim Company under the oversight of its Founder & Chairman — ensuring the Foundation's mission remains permanently insulated from commercial pressure.
