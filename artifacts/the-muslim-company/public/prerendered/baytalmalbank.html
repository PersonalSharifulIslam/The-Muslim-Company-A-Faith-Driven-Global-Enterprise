Historical Foundation

What is Bayt Al-Mal?

Bayt al-Mal (بيت المال) — "House of Wealth" or "House of Money" — is an institution rooted in the earliest foundations of Islamic civilization. Established during the era of the Rightly-Guided Caliphs, it served as the public treasury of the Islamic state: collecting revenues, distributing zakat, funding public welfare, and managing the financial affairs of the Muslim community with justice and accountability.

Inspired by this noble legacy, The Bayt Al-Mal Bank seeks to revive the spirit of Prophetic economic justice — transforming banking from a tool of exploitation into an instrument of human dignity, societal welfare, and civilizational advancement.

Core Pillars

Shariah Compliance

Every product, service, and transaction strictly adheres to Islamic financial law — eliminating riba (interest), gharar (excessive uncertainty), and all prohibited instruments.

Wealth Justice

Inspired by the Prophetic model of Bayt al-Mal, wealth is treated as an amanah (trust) — distributed equitably, invested ethically, and directed toward societal benefit.

Global Reach

Operating across borders with a vision to serve Muslim communities, ethical entrepreneurs, and mission-driven institutions worldwide through Shariah-compliant financial solutions.

Institutional Integrity

Built on a foundation of transparency, accountability, and strong corporate governance — aligned with both Islamic ethical standards and international regulatory frameworks.

Sustainable Finance

Prioritizing investments in renewable energy, ethical manufacturing, humanitarian development, and socially responsible enterprises that generate long-term positive impact.

Community Development

Supporting zakat distribution, waqf management, microfinance for underserved communities, and charitable endowments that strengthen Muslim societies worldwide.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is The Bayt Al-Mal Bank open for customers yet?

Not yet. The Bayt Al-Mal Bank is currently in development, building its Shariah governance framework and pursuing regulatory licensing before public launch.

When will The Bayt Al-Mal Bank launch?

The Bayt Al-Mal Bank is targeting its first public launch in 2031, following the completion of its Shariah governance framework and regulatory licensing process.

Who is behind The Bayt Al-Mal Bank?

The Bayt Al-Mal Bank is founded and led by Shariful Islam, Founder & CEO, as a division of The Muslim Company.

How is this different from a conventional bank?

Unlike conventional banks, The Bayt Al-Mal Bank operates entirely free of riba (interest), using Shariah-compliant contracts such as Murabahah, Musharakah, and Mudarabah for all financial products.
